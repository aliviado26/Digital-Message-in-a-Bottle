# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want to send an anonymous thought into a persistent shared world, then return over time to watch what the ocean does with it. Players can also explore the ocean, receive sealed messages, rescue stranded bottles, and discover real-world cleanup opportunities.

## Product Purpose

Digital Message in a Bottle turns asynchronous anonymous messaging into a slow, ocean-driven game. Success means a player can write, seal, release, drift, receive, explore, rescue, and relocate without the destination ever becoming predictable or user-selected.

## Positioning

Messages do not go to a chosen or randomly assigned recipient. A server-owned simulation moves sealed bottles through modeled currents until the ocean carries them to a shore, strands them, or lets another player rescue them.

## Operating Context

The product is a responsive browser experience used in short return visits: players check their shore, inspect the chart, release a message, look for arrivals, or continue a long-running voyage. The primary experience should feel like one playable board rather than a conventional collection of dashboard pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Supabase authentication and data remain the source of truth.
- A bottle costs one Fee to release; receiving and rescuing can earn Fees.
- Bottle age is real, and current boosts change movement rather than time.
- Only the final recipient can break a seal; explorers and rescuers cannot read it.
- Users choose a fixed Home Shore but never a bottle destination.
- Destination Progress unlocks the first relocation; later relocations cost Fees.
- Current Coins come from discovering real cleanup opportunities and temporarily accelerate currents.
- Exact user locations and personal information are not public.
- Existing detail routes remain available for deep actions, but the home surface is the single overview and primary game board.

## Brand Commitments

The name is **Digital Message in a Bottle**. The core line is: “Write something. Seal it. Throw it into the ocean. Then let go.” The experience should be indie, game-like, oceanic, hand-made, sketchy, and intentionally messy. `images/image.png` is binding layout and type-density reference; `images/3ecdb7c429a128b9a3d4fcf1a9393c83.jpg` is binding art-direction reference.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product lifecycle and economy are documented in `PLAN.md` and `DESC.md`.
- Working routes and Supabase actions cover release, drift, receipt, rescue, relocation, cleanup discovery, and boosts.
- Two user-provided visual references exist in `images/`.
- No testimonials, commercial claims, or public usage metrics are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- The ocean decides.
- Waiting and uncertainty are part of the experience.
- The seal matters.
- Exploration helps a message continue; it does not steal the message.
- The digital ocean should lead people back toward care for the real one.
